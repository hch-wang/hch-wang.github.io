## Summary
Implemented a simulation to automatically select action sets that can lead to fractal invariant measure.

## Example
Consider a simple random dynamical system on a torus
<p style="text-align:center">
$$
{x_{n + 1}} = \left\{ \begin{array}{l}
{f_0}({x_n}) = 4{x_n}\bmod \,1\\
{f_i}({x_n}) = \frac{x_n + i - 1}{16}
\end{array} \right.
$$
</p>
i is ranging from 1,...,16. And there is 1/2 probability to choose $$f_0$$ and $$1/32$$ probability to choose any $$f_i$$.

Consider $$\omega = a_0a_1a_2...a_N, a_i=0,...,16$$ is a symbolic sequence, and 
$$f_{\omega} = f_{a_N} \circ ... \circ f_{a_0}$$.

For a set of sampling from the uniform distribution on square, consider the 
probability measure of $$(f_{\omega}(x),f_{\omega}(y))$$.
